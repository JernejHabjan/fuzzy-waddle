# Trump Defense 2016

Trump Defense 2016 is a tongue-in-cheek tower defense game about holding the line at the border. Build up the wall, place defenses, and stop incoming ground and flying units before they break through. Manage your cash and lives, and upgrade your defenses as each battlefield brings a new challenge.

## The campaign

- **Red, White & Boom** introduces ground units and cannon defenses.
- **Stars After Dark** adds flying attackers, sniper towers, and a night battlefield lit by spotlights.
- **Liberty's Last Stand** mixes the threats and asks you to choose your building sites carefully.

The campaign has three levels. Completing a level unlocks the next one, and progress is saved in your browser.

## How to play

Choose a level, follow its short briefing, then use the controls to defend the map:

- Select a building site and place a cannon or, when available, a sniper tower.
- Select one of your defenses to upgrade it.
- Raise the wall to keep attackers from breaking through.
- Watch your cash, remaining lives, and wall height as the battle unfolds.

Keyboard shortcuts are **B** for sniper tower, **C** for cannon, **U** to upgrade, **Space** to raise the wall, and **P** to pause. You can also use the on-screen buttons.

## About the project

Trump Defense began as a 2016 school project for the Computer Graphics class at the Faculty of Computer and Information Science, University of Ljubljana. It pairs a satirical campaign presentation with the familiar strategy of choosing defenses, managing resources, and protecting a route.
