What makes it believable?

  • Scouting answers strategic questions; repeated sightings aren't invented armies.
  • Timed attacks and sensible retreats—not endless preparation or reckless chasing.
  • Real progress deadlines: income delivered, forces ready, objectives contested.
  • 121 planned scenario IDs, including faults and continuous matches; not 121 passing tests yet.

What is—and isn't—in scope?

  • 16 implementation stages: focused checks throughout, extensive validation at Stage 15.
  • Both playable factions using their actual supported units and maps.
  • Walls, towers, stairs and legal boats/air combat. No imaginary naval roster.
  • Gates and flying cargo units stay future capabilities until real definitions exist.
Blueprint, not a benchmark. The finished AI must prove these behaviors through actual gameplay and reproducible tests.
